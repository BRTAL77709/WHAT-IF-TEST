What if money actually grew on trees? One leaf could change everything.

At first, people would race to harvest a fortune.

Then more trees bloom. Soon, cash is everywhere.

Everyone can spend more. But the shelves have not grown.

More money cannot instantly create more bread, homes, or workers.

With the same goods and far more cash, prices could soar.

A mountain of banknotes might barely buy a single loaf.

Real wealth comes from growing food, building homes, and delivering what people need.

Even a millionaire could face an empty shop, and go home hungry.

Would you want this world? Tell us: yes or no.
