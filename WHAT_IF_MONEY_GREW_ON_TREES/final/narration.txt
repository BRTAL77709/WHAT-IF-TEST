What if money actually grew on trees?

At first, everyone would think they had just become rich.

Then every tree on Earth starts producing cash.

Suddenly, almost everyone has more money than they can spend.

But there is one problem. More money does not mean more food, houses, or resources.

As cash becomes unlimited, prices could rise faster and faster.

Eventually, those piles of money might barely buy anything.

Because real wealth is not just money. It is the things people can actually produce.

The richest person in this world might still be unable to buy dinner.

Would you still want money to grow on trees? Tell us: yes or no?
